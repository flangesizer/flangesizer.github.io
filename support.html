Help and support

Last updated: 28 October 2026

How to use Flange Sizer

  1. Confirm you are 18 or older and measuring yourself, then tap Get started.
  2. Choose Left or Right, then take a photo or choose one from your library.
  3. Check that the yellow frame sits on the card’s edges. Drag the yellow corners if it doesn’t.
  4. Tap the nipple once. A green outline and a pink line across it appear.
  5. Check that the pink line runs straight across the nipple, from edge to edge near its base (not including the areola). Drag the pink dots if needed. The number updates as you drag.
  6. Tap Confirm. Repeat for the other side if you like.
  7. See your suggested flange size and the range to try. You can email, share or download a short text summary.

Tips for a good photo

  • Use any standard bank, ID or loyalty card (credit-card size). Put the back or a plain side up.
  • Hold the card flat under the nipple so the nipple rests naturally on it. Hold the card by its far end so your fingers don’t cover the corners.
  • Hold the phone straight above, parallel to the card, about 20–30 cm (8–12 in) away.
  • Keep all four card corners in the photo.
  • Use even light and avoid glare on the card.
  • Measure before pumping, since the nipple can swell during pumping.

Questions

Is my photo uploaded or saved?

No. The photo is measured on your phone and is never uploaded. Flange Sizer doesn’t save it, and it’s gone when you close the app. See the privacy policy.

Why do I need a card?

A standard card is always the same size (85.6 × 54 mm), so it works as a ruler in the photo. It also gives a plain background that makes the nipple easier to see.

The card wasn’t found, or the frame is in the wrong place.

Drag the four corners onto the card’s corners yourself. If that’s hard, retake the photo with all four corners visible, a plain side up and no glare.

The outline is wrong, or the number looks off.

Drag the two pink dots onto the edges of the nipple. If you can’t get a clear view, retake the photo from straight above in even light. Results are estimates and can be off by a few mm, so always check the fit while pumping.

My size is between two flange sizes. Which do I pick?

Flange Sizer suggests one size and a range. Many people try the suggested size first, then compare with the next size. The right fit is the one where the nipple moves freely in the tunnel, little areola is pulled in, and pumping doesn’t hurt. Flange inserts can make a larger flange smaller.

Pumping still hurts.

Stop and talk to a lactation consultant (IBCLC), midwife or doctor. Flange Sizer is a sizing aid, not medical advice, and can’t tell why something hurts.

Can I measure someone else?

No. Flange Sizer is for adults (18+) measuring themselves only.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The app does all its work on your phone and doesn’t need a connection.

How do I delete my data?

There is nothing to delete: Flange Sizer doesn’t keep your photos or measurements. Summaries you emailed, shared or downloaded are in the place you sent them, and you can delete them there.

Contact and reporting a problem

Email flangesizer.app@gmail.com. To help us look into a problem, please include:

  • your phone model and its iOS or Android version;
  • the Flange Sizer version (shown in your app store’s page for the app);
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.

Please don’t send photos of yourself. A description is enough. If a picture would help, send a screenshot that shows only the card or the error message.

We aim to reply within a few days. Support is offered on a best-effort basis.
