Flange Sizer

Find a breast pump flange size to try, from one photo.

A free sizing aid for pumping parents. A bank card works as the ruler.

How it works

  1. 1Rest your nipple on a bank card and take a photo from straight above.
  2. 2Tap the nipple. Flange Sizer outlines it and estimates its width.
  3. 3Check the outline, drag the dots if needed, and confirm. You get a suggested flange size and a range to try for each side.

Private by design

Your photo is measured on your phone. It is never uploaded or saved, and there are no accounts, analytics or ads. Read the privacy policy.

Good to know

Results are estimates and can be off by a few mm, so always check the fit while pumping. Flange Sizer is for adults (18+) measuring themselves. It is not medical advice: if pumping hurts, see a lactation consultant (IBCLC).
