1. What Flange Sizer is

Flange Sizer is a free sizing aid. It helps you estimate your nipple diameter from a photo, using a standard bank card as a ruler, and suggests breast pump flange sizes to try. You check the outline and measurement on screen, adjust it if needed, and confirm it yourself.

3. Results are estimates

Measurements are estimates and can be off by a few millimeters, for example because of the angle of the photo, lighting, or how the card was held. Bodies also change during the day and over time. You are responsible for checking the result, and for choosing and trying a flange size. Always check the fit while pumping: the nipple should move freely in the tunnel, little areola should be pulled in, and pumping should not hurt.
