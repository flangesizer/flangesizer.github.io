Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy covers the Flange Sizer app for iPhone and Android and the Flange Sizer web page. Both work the same way.

In short

Flange Sizer does not collect, upload, store or share your photos or measurements. Your photo is measured on your own device and is discarded when you leave the screen or close the app. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Your photo

  • The photo you take or choose is processed only on your device, in memory.
  • It is never uploaded. Flange Sizer has no server, and the app and page are built so they cannot send data over the internet.
  • Flange Sizer does not save it. Closing the app or page, or starting a new measurement, discards it. A photo you chose from your library stays in your library, as before.

Camera and photo access

The app asks for access to your camera or photo library only so you can take or pick the photo to measure. It reads only the one photo you choose. You can turn these permissions off at any time in your phone’s settings and still type in a measurement by hand.

Your measurements

The measurements and flange sizes exist only on screen while you use Flange Sizer; they are not saved. They leave your device only if you press Email, Share or Download. These create a short text summary (never the photo) and hand it to the mail app, app or place you choose. From then on, that app’s own privacy policy applies.

No tracking

No cookies, accounts, analytics, advertising, crash-reporting tools or other third-party services are built into Flange Sizer.

App stores and website hosting

  • Apple and Google handle app downloads. Depending on your device settings, they may give developers anonymous, aggregated statistics (for example, the number of downloads or crash counts). We do not use these to identify anyone. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, when you visit a page. We have no access to them. GitHub’s privacy policy applies.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you, and we delete it when it is no longer needed to help you. Please never send photos of yourself.

Who it is for

Flange Sizer is for adults (18+) measuring themselves only. It is not meant for children. Never photograph anyone under 18, or anyone without their consent.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or keep your photos or measurements, there is nothing about you for us to look up, correct or delete. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to delete that email at any time.

Not medical advice

Results are estimates to help you choose a flange size, and they can be off by a few millimeters. They are not a diagnosis or medical advice. If you have pain or concerns, see a lactation consultant (IBCLC) or another health professional.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting any data, this policy will be updated before that happens.

Contact

Questions about privacy: flangesizer.app@gmail.com
